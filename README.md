## Initial retail pricing benchmark
On 28 September 2026, the catalogue was given launch-placeholder retail prices using current UK microgreen sellers as benchmarks. Core 100g products sit mainly around £3.25–£4.95, while slower/specialist micro herbs are higher. These are competitive starting prices for presentation and basket testing, not an assertion of Alex's final approved prices.

Useful benchmarks used included:
- Axholme Produce: pea shoots 100g £3.00; broccoli, red cabbage, radish and microgreen mixes listed around £3.50.
- Vittoria Greens: pea and sunflower 100g about £3.50–£3.99; healthy mix 100g about £3.50–£3.99; immunity mix 100g about £4.75–£5.00; premium smaller packs are higher.
- Shoot Farm: micro salad mix £2.90 for 70g.
- Shedload of Greens: pea/sunflower £2.50 for 70g; salad mix £2.50 for 75g; specialist herbs/radishes command more per gram.
- Fine Food Specialist: specialist micro herbs such as coriander, basil, amaranth, celery and sorrel are typically sold in much smaller premium packs, supporting higher equivalent 100g prices.

## Alex approval placeholders
The logo/brand, company story, contact details, address, delivery area, food-safety statements, sustainability claims and payment provider are intentionally not invented. Prices are researched starting points and can be changed before launch.

## Google Sites note
The connected Google Drive tools cannot directly edit or publish Google Sites. The existing public site was therefore left intact. This package is the replacement source ready for a Sites-capable editor or another hosting surface.
