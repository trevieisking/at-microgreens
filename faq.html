Answers for the preview site. Operational details are intentionally conservative until Alex confirms them.

What are microgreens?

Microgreens are young edible seedlings harvested early in their growth. Different varieties offer different flavours, colours and textures.

How should I store microgreens?

Storage instructions will be confirmed for each product before launch. Refrigerated storage is common for many harvested microgreens.

How long do microgreens last?

Shelf life depends on the variety, harvest condition, packaging and refrigeration. A.T will publish product-specific guidance before sales begin.
